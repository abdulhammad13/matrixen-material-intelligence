DETAILED NOTICE INVITING EXPRESSION OF INTEREST (EOI) 
EOI No.: NTPC/QA&I /EOI/ 2/2026 Date: 11-03-2026 
NTPC Ltd. invites Expression of Interest (EOI) from domestic vendors / Training 
Institutes for conducting Welder Training Programs at NTPC Projects/ Stations aimed 
at skill development and competency enhancement of personnel. 
1.0 Objective: 
a) This EOI is to identify interested domestic vendors / Training Institutes for providing
Welder Training Programs at NTPC Projects/ Stations for Structural steel, Low
pressure and High-pressure welding training programs in the following welding
processes including IBR Certification:
i. SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding)
ii. GTAW (TIG Welding)
iii. GMAW (MIG Welding)
b) Provide training as per relevant codes such as:
a. ASME Section IX
b. AWS D1.1
c) Conduct training at NTPC projects/ Stations.
d) Provide training material, study modules and assessment tools.
e) Conduct theoretical training by experienced instructors.
f) Conduct practical sessions with adequate machines, support manpower,
consumables, materials for practical training and safety equipment.
g) Conduct welder qualification tests (WQT) and maintain proper documentation.
h) Issue certificates upon successful completion of the training.
i) Ensure compliance with safety standards during training.
j) The applicant is required to provide the detail of the training modules for which they
have been accredited from the certified body (if any).
k) Based on response of EOI, a database of such domestic vendors/ training institutes
will be prepared, and possibility will be explored to provide opportunity to such
domestic vendors/ training institutes to provide training at NTPC Projects/stations.
l) As per the response of EOI, detailed discussion may be done with such applicants
(domestic vendors/ training institutes) for technical and commercial terms and
condition. Further, techno-commercial proposal may be sought for providing the
certified training.
