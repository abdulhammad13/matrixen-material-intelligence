- GST is a statutory tax component, deposited with the Government

Employer and need not be secured through BG.
Request / Proposal: We request NTPC to kindly exclude GST amount
from the Bank Guarantee coverage and retain only 110% of the
advance value (excluding GST), in line with standard practice
followed in NTPC and other PSU EPC contracts.
This will avoid unnecessary enhancement of BG value, optimize
contractor’s bank limits and maintain compliance with the intent of the
procurement guidelines.

which state clearly that Submission of an irrevocable 
bank guarantee for the amount equivalent to 110% of

Schedule of Release of Advance
As per tender provisions, the interest-free advance of 6% of Contract
Price shall be released in four trenches of 1.5% each, due in the 9th,
14th, 19th, and 24th month from the date of LOA. Further, 80% of
progressive payments are released against RA bills, and an optional
4% interest-bearing advance is available.
We would like to NTPC’s attention that this release structure creates a
cash-flow gap during the initial and intermediate stages because:
- The first trench of interest-free advance becomes available only in
the 9th month, whereas major mobilization expenses occur during the
first 3–6 months (site setup, shuttering, reinforcement, slip-form
equipment, batching plants, cranes, etc.).
- The 80% progressive billing cap limits net cash inflow during peak
execution months.
- If the bidder chooses not to avail the 4% interest-bearing advance,
there is no effective working capital cushion in the early project phase.
We request you to:
(a) Kindly reconsider the schedule of advance release and allow
immediate disbursement of full 6% interest-free advance at project
start, upon submission of BG, to ensure smooth mobilization.
(b) Alternatively, permit early milestone-linked release (e.g., 2% on
site mobilization, 2% on foundation start, 2% on slip-form readiness).
(c) In absence of early advance, consider raising progressive payment
release limit from 80% to 90% of executed work value to balance cash
flow.
In view of above, with the current staggered advance (9th–24th
month) and 80% RA billing limit, contractor cash flow becomes
negative for the first several quarters, affecting timely execution and
resource mobilization.
Provision of Tender Document shall prevail.
