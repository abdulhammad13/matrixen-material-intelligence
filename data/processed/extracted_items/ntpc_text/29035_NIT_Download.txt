7.1.1.  The Bidder should have successfully executed Bituminous  Road construction works/ 
Bituminous Road repair works in India in preceding Seven (07) y ears as on the date of 
Techno-Commercial bid opening as per any of the following criteria: 
 
a) One work of executed value not less than Rs 51 Lakh. 
 
OR 
 
b) Two works of executed value not less than Rs 31 Lakh each. 
 
OR 
c) Three works of executed value not less Rs 25 Lakh each 
Note for clause: 7.1.1 of QR: 
(i) The work “executed” menti oned above means the bidder should  have achieved the 
criteria specified above, even if the total contract is not completed / closed.  
(ii) In case of orders which includes other items also are unde r execution, only the executed 
value of ‘Bituminous Road construction works / Bituminous Road repair works’ as certified 
by the Client shall be considered. 
(iii) The above value of executed Work(s) is exclusive of applicable tax/ GST. 
(iv) The reference works execut ed by the bidder’s group company  / subsidiary 
company/holding company shall not  be considered for meeting the  qualifying 
requirements by the bidder.  
7.2.0 Financial Criteria: 
7.2.1  The average annual turnover of the Bidder in the precedi ng three (3) financial years as on 
the date of techno-commercial bid opening should not be less than Rs. 63.00 Lakh (Rupees 
Sixty-Three Lakh only). 
7.2.2  While computing the annual  turnover, other income shall not be considered. 
7.2.3  In case, where audited results for the last financial year as on the date of Techno- commercial 
bid opening are not available, the financial results certified by a practicing Chartered 
Accountant shall be considered acceptable. 
 
In case, Bidder is not able to submit the certificate from prac ticing Chartered Accountant 
certifying its financial parameters, the audited results of thr ee consecutive financial years 
preceding the last financial year shall be considered for evaluating the financial parameters.

bidding documents stating that the financial results of the company are under audit as on the 
date of techno commercial bid opening and the certificate from the practicing Chartered 
Account certifying the financial parameters is not available.
