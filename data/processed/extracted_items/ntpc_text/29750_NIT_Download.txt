2.0 BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK 
The scope of this contract shall comprise of deployment and operation of Advance Life 
Support (ALS) Ambulance as per Ambulance Code AIS- 125 of brand new, in white colour, 
air-conditioned (TEMP 18-25° C), BS-VI compliant, tubeless tyres, manufactured and 
fabricated as per Ambulance Code AIS-125 only along with equipment as per Ambulance 
code AIS-125 for ambulance of “category-D”, consumables, fuel and manpower. 
NTPC intends to finance subject Package through Internal Resources.

5.0 QUALIFYING REQUIREMENTS FOR BIDDERS. 
 
The bidder who wishes to participate in the bidding shall meet the Qualifying Requirements 
stipulated hereunder: 
 
5.1.0 Technical Criteria: 
 
5.1.1 The bidder should have executed “deployment of at least one Ambulance on a 24x7 
basis for any ‘Organisation /Hospital / Medical Centre’ for a continuous period of not 
less than one (1) year in a single contract” within the preceding seven (7) years prior 
to the date of Techno-Commercial bid opening. 
5.1.2    The bidder should have executed the work(s) of “managing / providing /deployment” 
of Ambulance(s) within the preceding seven (7) years prior to the date of Techno-
Commercial bid opening, in any of the following manner; 
 
a. One (1) order having executed value not less than Rs. 39 lakh 
                                               OR 
b. Two (2) orders having executed value not less than Rs. 25 lakh each. 
                                               OR 
c. Three (3) orders having executed value not less than Rs. 20 lakh each. 
 
 
     Notes for Technical Criteria:  
 
Notes for Clause 5.0 above: 
1. The word “Executed" mentioned above means that the bidder should have achieved the 
criteria specified above, even if the total contract is started earlier and/or is not 
completed/closed. 
2. In case of orders under execution, the value of work executed prior to the date of Techno- 
Commercial bid opening duly certified by bidder’s client shall be considered acceptable. 
3. Reference work executed by the bidder as a sub-contractor may also be considered 
provided the certificate issued by main contractor is duly certified by owner specifying the 
scope of work executed by the sub-contractor in support of qualifying requirements. 
4. "For arriving at the executed value of work specified above, basic amount only shall be 
considered. In case contract is inclusive of taxes, bidder should provide the break – up of 
basic value and taxes. 
5. For arriving at the executed value of work specified above, cost of fuel charges would not 
be considered, if reimbursable in the reference contract. 
6. Multiple orders placed against single “rate contract / Agreement / MoU” shall be treated as 
one work for the above purpose. 
 
5.2.  Financial criteria: 
5.2.1 The Average Annual Financial Turnover (AATO) of the bidder during preceding three 
(03) consecutive financial years as on the date of Techno - Commercial bid openingshould not 
be less than Rs. 49 Lakh (Rupees forty-nine lakh only). 
 
5.2.2 In case the Bidder does not satisfy the financial criteria, stipulated above at 5.2.1  
on its own, its Holding Company would be required to meet the stipulated turnover 
requirements at Cl 5.2.1 above, provided that the Net Worth of such Holding Company as on 
the last day of the preceding financial year is at least equal to or more than the paid-up share 
capital of the Holding Company. In such an event, the Bidder would be required to furnish 
along with its Techno-Commercial bid, a Letter of Undertaking from the Holding Company, 
supported by the Holding Company’s Board Resolution, as per the format enclosed in the Bid 
Documents, pledging unconditional & irrevocable financial support for the execution of the 
Contract by the Bidder in case of award. 
 
5.2.3   In case the bidder is not able to furnish its audited financial statements on standalone 
entity basis, the unaudited unconsolidated financial statements of the bidder can be 
considered acceptable provided the bidder further furnishes the following documents for 
substantiation of its qualification:’ 
a)   Copies of the unaudited unconsolidated financial statements of the bidder along with 
copies of the audited consolidated financial statements of the Holding Company. 
b)  A certificate from the CEO/CFO of the Holding Company, as per the format enclosed in 
the bidding documents, stating that the unaudited unconsolidated financial statements 
form part of the Consolidated Annual Financial Statements of the Holding Company. 
5.2.4 In cases where audited results for the last preceding financial year as on the date of 
Techno-Commercial bid opening are not available, the financial results certified by a 
practicing Chartered Accountant shall be considered acceptable. In case, Bidder is not 
able to submit the Certificate from practicing Chartered Accountant certifying its 
financial parameters, the audited results of three consecutive financial years 
preceding the last financial year shall be considered for evaluating the financial 
parameters. Further, a Certificate would be required from the CEO/CFO as per the 
format enclosed in the bidding documents stating that the financial results of the 
Company are under audit as on the date of Techno-Commercial bid opening and the 
Certificate from the practicing Chartered Accountant certifying the financial 
parameters is not available. 
Notes for 5.2.0 above: 
1. Net worth means the sum total of the paid-up share capital and free reserves. Free reserve 
means all reserves credited out of the profits and share premium account but does not 
include reserves credited out of the revaluation of the assets, write back of depreciation 
provision and amalgamation. Further any debit balance of Profit and Loss account and 
miscellaneous expenses to the extent not adjusted or written off, if any, shall be reduced from 
reserves and surplus. 
2. Other income shall not be considered for arriving at annual turnover figures. 
3. “Holding Company” and “Subsidiary Company” shall have the meaning ascribed to them as 
per Companies Act of India.

SCOPE OF WORK, TERMS AND CONDITIONS 
Name of Work: Deployment and Operations of Advanced Life Support ( ALS) Ambulance for a 
period of 5 years at Jivantika Hospital MTPS Kanti. 
SCOPE OF WORK: 
1. Deployment and Operation of Advance Life Support (ALS) Ambulance as per Ambulance 
Code AIS- 125 of brand new , in white colour, air-conditioned (TEMP 18-25° C), BS-VI 
compliant, tubeless tyres, manufactured and fabricated as per Ambulance Code AIS-125 only 
along with equipment as per Ambulance code AIS-125 for ambulance of “ category-D”, 
consumables, fuel and manpower. The manpower to be available for 24 X 7 @ 8 hrs shift basis 
to carry and manage serious patients as and when required. They should work as per directions 
of the Chief Medical Officer (CMO) / Medical Officer In-Charge (MO I/C) / Engineer-in-Charge 
(EIC) or authorized representative, MTPS, Kanti. 
Manpower Requirement: 
 
Designation Manpower Category 
EMT 3 No’s  Skilled (SW)  
Medical Attendant  3 No’s  Semi-Skilled (SSW)  
Driver 3 No’s  Semi-Skilled (SSW)  
 
Note: 
 
1. 1 No. Reliever will be provided for 3 EMT’s 
2. 1 No. Reliever will be provided for 3 No’s Medical Attendant  
3. 1 No. Reliever will be provided for 3 Ambulance Drivers 
4. Total manpower mentioned is for 3 shifts- 24X7  
 
TERMS AND CONDITION OF ALS AMBULANCE: 
Advance Life Support (ALS) Ambulance: 
1. Advance Life Support (ALS) Ambulance of make Force traveller trauma or equivalent, 
Model -2026 or later (brand New), 3350- WB (8+d+1p, 54151x1975wx2550h) with power 
Steering, 16 V, DOHC, VGT, Dual Mass Fly Wheel in white colour, air-conditioned, BS-
VI compliant, tubeless tyres, manufactured and Fabricated as per Ambulance Code AIS-125 
only with 3-way siren & light, mike and operating system along with equipment as per AIS-
125 (Part-2), consumables, fuel (HSD) and manpower. i.e., Drivers, Emergency Medical 
Technicians & Medical Attendants.  
2. ‘Ambulance' should be written on front and back of the vehicle with fluorescent red colour 
stickers and ambulance logo in blue colour sticker.  
3. NTPC MTPS, Kanti name in Hindi/regional language and English should also be written 
on both side of the vehicle along with NTPC logo in bold letters.  
4. ALS ambulance shall be stationed in hospital for 24x7 and move to attend /transport 
patients as per the direction of the CMO of the hospital.  
5. Equipment/instruments & Other items to be made available and functional all the time. 
6. GPS enabled real time position tracker along with the software to track the same to be provided 
by the agency to the EIC/CMO. 
7. A total of 12 manpower (3+1 Drivers, 3+1 EMTs and 3+1 Medical Attendants) shall be 
deployed for 8-hour shifts as per relevant labour laws in which the three will be in shift and one 
will be reliever who will also be paid as per labour law. These manpower should undergo 
refresher course every year for equipment functioning and first aid which will be arranged and 
borne by the vendor. 
 
A. Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) For ALS Ambulance (3+1 EMT): 
1. Emergency medical tech. should have certificate in Emergency Medical Technician advance 
through recognized institute of “Healthcare Sector Skill Council of India” (HSSC) or any 
other government recognized institute with minimum one-year experience in Emergency 
Services. 
2. Ambulance services are associated with human life. Therefore, emergency medical 
technicians must have full Experience, Knowledge/Trained for operation of all the equipment 
in ALS ambulance. 
3. They should attend the patients in emergency and also associate with ambulance for safe pick 
and drop including shifting of patients in/out of the ambulance at site/hospital while 
transportation and as per instructions of CMO/medical officer in-charge/ engineer in-charge 
or authorized executive of the project hospital. 
4. They should also assist Doctors/Staff Nurses on duty for better management of the patients 
in emergency as well as in stabilization center and to work in rural health camps/dispensary 
outside of the hospital as per instructions of above authorities.
