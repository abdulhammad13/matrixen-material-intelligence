QUALIFYING REQUIREMENT 
Name of Work: PROVIDING AND FIXING FLOOR TILES IN QUARTERS ALLOTTED TO NSPCL R OURKELA 
 
1.1 Technical Criteria: 
1.1.1 The bidder should have executed the following similar works within the preceding 07 years 
as on the date of technical bid opening which fulfils either of the following criteria as follows:. 
1.1.2 (i) 01 (One) similar completed works costing not less than the amount equal to Rs. 79.28 lakh 
OR 
(ii) 02 (Two) similar completed works costing not less than the amount equal to Rs.49.55 
Lakh each. 
OR 
(iii) 03 (Three) similar completed works costing not less than the amount equal to Rs.39.64 
Lakh each 
Note (I) Similar work means: Civil maintenance or  Civil repair or  Civil Construction of 
buildings works. 
 
(II) Completed works means: The executed / completed portion of BOQ even if the work 
has not been completed in totality (Subject to furnishing Proof of executed value 
of the work in the form of copies of RA bills, Relevant PO copy and Client's 
Completion Certificate / Final Deviation Statement). 
 
(III) Contract for pure earth work/pure steel fabrication/pure RCC work/mechanical 
erection j ob/pure water supply & drainage piping work/pure aluminium work 
shall not be considered for qualification. 
 
(IV) Reference work executed by bidder as a sub -contractor may also be considered 
provided the certificate issued by the main contractor is duly certifie d by the 
Project Authority specifying the work executed by the sub -contractor in support 
of the qualifying requirements. 
 
(V) The bidder shall submit the Audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account for the 
specified period. In case where Audited results fo r the preceding FY are not 
available, certificate of financial statements from a practicing Chartered 
Accountant shall also be considered acceptable. In case, Bidder is not able to 
submit the Certificate from practicing Chartered Account certifying its fin ancial 
parameters, the audited results of three consecutive financial years preceding the 
last financial year shall be considered for evaluating the financial parameters. 
Further, a certificate would be required from CEO/CFO as per the format enclosed 
in t he bidding documents stating that the financial results of the company are 
under audit as on the date of Techno -commercial bid opening and the certificate 
from the practicing Chartered Accountant certifying the financial parameters is 
not available. 
(VI) Other income shall not be considered for arriving annual turnover. 
(VII) The figures indicated 1.1.2 & 1.2.1 are excluding Taxes & Duties.  
1.2 Financial Criteria 
1.2.1 The average annual turnover (AATO) of the bidder in the preceding three (03) financial years 
as on the date of Techno-Commercial bid opening shall not be less than Rs. 99.11 Lakh.
