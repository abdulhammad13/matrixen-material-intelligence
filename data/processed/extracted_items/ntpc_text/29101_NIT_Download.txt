QUALIFYING REQUIREMENT 
Name of Work :  Construction of Miscellaneous Shed in CHP Area at NSPCL CPP-II Durgapur  
 
The Bidders who wish to participate in the bidding shall satisfactorily establish th at they fulfil the following qualifying 
Requirements:- 
 
1.0 Financial Criteria 
1.1 
Turnover: The average annual turnover of the bidder, in the preceding 03(three) financial years as on date of 
techno-commercial bid opening , shall not be less than Rs.68.76 Lakhs( excluding taxes and duties) 
2.0 Technical Criteria 
2.1 The bidder should have executed similar works  within preceding 07 years reckoned as  on the date of technical 
bid opening with either of the following : 
a) Single order of  value not less than Rs. 82.51 Lakhs (excluding taxes and duties)  
   OR 
b) Two Orders of  value not less than Rs. 51.57 lakhs each  (excluding taxes and duties) 
   OR 
c) Three Orders of  value not less than Rs.41.26  Lakh each  (excluding taxes and duties) 
 
 Notes : 
a) The word “executed” means that the bidder should have achieved the progress specified in the QR , even 
if total contract/work order is not completed/ closed. The same shall be supported by documentary 
evidence issued by the owner/employer such as Completion certificate/Joint protocol/running/Final Bills 
etc. 
b) Similar Works means the bidder should have executed  any steel structural works with RCC(Reinforced 
cement concrete) or Civil Construction works or Civil Maintenance work successfully  
c) For financial Selection criteria, QR para 1.0, Bidder must submit copy of Audited Balance sheet and Profit 
& loss Account for the specified period. In case, where audited results for the last financial year as on the 
date of technical bid opening, ate not available, the financia l results  certified by a practicing charted 
accountant shall be considered acceptable. In case, Bidder is not able to submit the certificate from the 
practising charted accountant certifying its financial parameters, the audited results of three consecutiv e 
financial years preceding the last financial year, shall be considered for evaluating the financial 
parameters. 
d) Other income shall not be considered for calculation of Annual Turnover.
