Expression of Interest for Procurement of Solar Power from Operational NTPC Solar 
Plants through Open Access by Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Consumers  
 
1.0 NTPC Vidyut Vyapar Nigam Limited (NVVN), a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC 
Limited and a Category-I Power Trading Licensee, invites Expressions of Interest (EOI) 
from eligible Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Consumers, Bulk Consumers and Open 
Access Consumers interested in procurement of solar power from operational NTPC 
Solar Power Plants through Open Access. 
 
2.0 The objective of this EOI is to identify prospective consumers for procurement of solar 
power, understand their capacity requirements, preferred supply tenure, delivery 
locations and commercial preferences, and facilitate supply through mutually agreed 
contractual arrangements. 
 
3.0 Based on the responses received to this EOI, NVVN may identify prospective 
APPLICANTs and may enter into bilateral discussions with interested APPLICANTs 
for supply of solar power on mutually agreed commercial terms and conditions. NVVN 
may also, wherever considered appropriate, invite offers through an open or limited 
tendering process. 
  
4.0  Eligibility for Participation in EOI   
  
4.1    The following entities shall be eligible to participate in this EOI: 
a) Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Consumers, including but not limited to 
energy-intensive industries such as Aluminium, Fertilizer, Iron & Steel, Cement, 
Pulp & Paper, Chlor  Alkali, Sugar, Textile, Chemicals, Petroleum Refineries, 
Data Centres, IT Parks, Metro Rail, Railways, Airports, Ports, SEZs, Commercial 
Establishments, and other industrial or commercial consumers; 
b) Bulk Consumers and other eligible Open Access consumers;  
c) Distribution Companies, and 
d) Sole Proprietorships, Partnership Firms, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), 
Companies, Consortia, Cooperative Societies, Registered Trusts, or any other 
legally constituted entity having a valid GSTIN and PAN (where applicable under 
Indian law),

1.0   INTRODUCTION  
  
NTPC Vidyut Vyapar Nigam Ltd. (NVVN) is a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Limited, India’s 
largest power producer. NVVN was established with the objective of carrying out the business 
of Purchase / Sale of all forms of electrical power from SEBs, Power Distribution Companies, 
other organizations and bulk power consumers etc. NVVN is the only PSU specializing in 
power trading business, and, as per the latest CERC regulation, we hold the highest category 
‘I’ trading license. 

NTPC VIDYUT VYAPAR NIGAM LTD  
(A Wholly Owned Subsidiary of NTPC Ltd)  
   
Procurement of Solar Power from Operational NTPC Solar Plants through Open Access by Commercial & 
Industrial (C&I) Consumers, Distribution Companies and others 
NVVN has access to a diversified portfolio of power, including renewable energy generated 
from NTPC's Solar Power Projects, and is well-positioned to offer reliable, competitively priced 
and sustainable green power solutions to consumers across the country through the applicable 
Open Access framework. 
NVVN invites Expressions of Interest (EOI) from eligible Commercial & Industrial (C&I) 
Consumers, Bulk Consumers, Distribution Licensees and Open Access Buyers across India 
who are interested in procuring renewable power from NTPC Solar Power Projects  under 
Short-Term, Medium-Term or Long-Term contracts depending upon consumer requirements . 
The objective of this EOI is to assess market demand and identify prospective consumers for 
supply of renewable energy under mutually agreed commercial terms.
