1.0 Bangladesh- India Friendship Power Company (Pvt.) Limited (“ BIFPCL”), acting as the 
Employer, invites sealed bid from eligible bidders for Condenser Tube Cleaning by High-
Pressure Water Jet & Bullet Shot Method  of 2x660 MW Maitree Super Thermal Power 
Project (“2x660 MW Maitree STPP” or “MSTPP”) (the “Project”) at Rampal, following Single 
Stage Two Envelope bidding procedure (Envelope-1: Technical Proposal and Envelope-2: 
Financial Proposal), as per the Scope of Work mentioned in brief hereinafter. 
 
2.0 Brief Scope of Work 
 
The scope of work and services, shall include the cleaning the condenser tube by high 
pressure water jet and bullet shot method. Each Condenser has 23452 Nos of tubes, and 
each unit has two condensers. The awarded party has to execute both cleaning method. 
The detailed Scope of Work is provided in the Technical Specification (Section-IV) of the 
Bidding Document. Bidder shall consider the Bidding Document in its entirety for complete 
understanding and interpretation of the Scope of Work and Services and shall submit a 
Proposal that meets the complete requirement of the Bidding Document.

5.0 Qualifying Requirements for Bidder 
 
5.1 Technical Criteria: 
 
5.1.1 The bidder should have experience in high pressure jet cleaning and bullet cleaning of 
Condensers of Thermal power plants of 100 MW or, above capacity  
 
and 
 
 
5.1.2 The bidder must have individually executed condenser tube cleaning by high pressure 
water jet and bullet shot method in any Power plant/refinery / any Govt. / Semi Govt. / 
Private Firm / Industry within five years preceding the date of techno-commercial bid 
opening,  of value not less than  
 
 
i) BDT 46.81 lacs in a single contract  
 
or 
 
ii) BDT 35.11 lacs each in at least two contracts  
 
or 
 
iii) BDT 23.40 lacs each in at least three contracts 
 
 Notes to clause 5.1 (Technical Criteria):  Tenderer has to submit all supporting 
credentials like PO/ WO / COA/ Experience certificates in support of technical criteria. 
 
The word “executed” means the Tenderer should have completed the work for the values 
specified  in the above QR within five years preceding the date of techno-commercial bid 
opening even if the contract period has been started earlier than the above period and / or is 
not completed as on the date of techno-commercial bid opening. 
 
5.2 Financial Criteria:  Tenderer shall have the following minimum level of financial capacity 
to qualify for the performance of the Supply under the Tender. 
 
The Average Annual Turnover of the Bidder, in the immediately preceding three financial 
years ending on (last financial year end date) should not be less than BDT 29.26 lacs. 
 
 
Notes to Clause 5.2 (Financial Criteria):  In support of and in order to establish the AAT as per 
Clause 5.2.1, the bidder shall submit the audited copy of ‘Annual Financial Report/ Balance 
Sheet/Profit & Loss Statement’ of relevant financial years.  
 
a) In cases where audited financial results for the immediately preceding financial years 
are not available, certification of the financial statements from a practicing Chartered 
Accountant shall also be considered acceptable. In case, the bidder is not able to submit the 
certificate from practicing Chartered Accountant certifying the financial results of the 
immediately preceding financial year, the audited financial results of preceding three 
consecutive financial years may be submitted and the same shall be considered for 
evaluating the financial parameters. Further, a Certificate would be required from the 
CEO/CFO as per the format enclosed in the bidding documents stating that the Financial 
results of the Company for immediately preceding Financial years are under audit and the 
Certificate would be required from the practicing Chartered Accountant certifying the 
financial parameters is not available. Bidder must also submit the copy of income tax return 
for the relevant years. 
 
b) Other income of bidder shall not be considered for arriving at the annual turnover.
