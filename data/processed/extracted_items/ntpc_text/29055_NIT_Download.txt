5.0 QUALIFYING REQUIREMENTS FOR BIDDERS: 
 
In addition to the requirements stipulated in Secti on ITB (Instructions to Bidders), the Bidder shall 
also meet the Qualifying Requirements stipulated here under:  
 
5.1 The bidder should have executed “Water proofing” works of at least 3500 Sqm, 
cumulatively, in not more than 3 contracts, within the prece ding seven (7) years prior to 
the date of Techno-Commercial bid opening. 
 
5.2 The bidder should have executed “Civil Maintenance”/ “Civil Repairs”/ “Civil 
Construction”/ “Water Proofing” works within the preceding seven (7) years prior to the 
date of Techno-Commercial bid opening meeting the following criteria: - 
 
i. Single order having executed value not less than Rs 104 Lakhs.   
OR 
ii.  Two orders having executed value not less than Rs 65 Lakhs each.  
OR 
iii. Three orders having executed value not less than Rs. 52 Lakhs e ach. 
   
Notes for Clause 5.0 above: 
 
1. The word “Executed" mentioned above means that the bidder should have achieved the 
criteria specified above, even if the total contract is started earlier and/or is not 
completed/closed. 
 
2. In case of orders under executi on, the val ue of work executed prior to the date of 
Techno-Commercial bid opening duly certified by bidder’s client shall be considered 
acceptable. 
 
3. Reference work executed by the bidder as a sub -contractor may also be considered 
provided the certificate i ssued by main contractor is duly certified by owner specifying 
the scope of work executed by the sub -contractor in support of qualifying requirements.  
 
4. For arriving at the executed value of work specified above, basic amount only shall be 
considered. In case contract is inclusive of taxes, bidder should provide the break –up of 
basic value and taxes. 
 
6.0 Technical Criteria:  
 
6.1 The Average Annual Financial Turnover (AATO) of the bidder during preceding three 
(03) consecutive financial years as on the date of Tech no - Commercial bid opening 
should not be less than Rs. 131 Lakhs (One Crore and Thirty -One Lakhs only). 
 
6.2 In case the Bidder does not satisfy the financial criteria, stipulated at Cl. No. 6.1 above 
on its own, its Holding Company would be required to meet the stipula ted turnover 
requirements at Cl. No. 6.1 above, provided that the Net Worth of such Holding 
Company as on the last day of the preceding financial year is at least equal to or more 
than the paid -up share capital of the Holding Company. In such an  event, the Bidder 
would be required to furnish along with its Techno-Commercial bid, a Letter of Undertaking 
from the Holding Company, supported by the Holding Company’s Board Resolution, as 
per the format enclosed in the Bid Documents, pledging unconditi onal & irre vocable 
financial support for the execution of the contract by the b idder in case of award. 
 
6.3 In case the bidder is not able to furnish its audited financial statements on stand -alone 
entity basis, the unaudited unconsolidated financial statement s of the bidder can be 
considered acceptable provided the bidder further furnishes the following documents for 
substantiation of its qualification:  
 Copies of the unaudited unconsolidated financial statements of the bidder along with 
copies of the audited consolidated financial statements of the Holding Company.  
 
 A certificate from the CEO/CFO of the Holding Company, as per the format enclosed 
in the bidding documents, stating that the unaudited unconsolidated financial 
statements form part of the Consolidat ed Annual Financial Statements of the Holding 
Company. 
 
6.4 In cases  where audited results for the last financial year  as on the date of Techno -
commercial bid opening are not available, the financial results  certified by a practicing 
Chartered Accountant  shall be conside red acceptable. In case, Bidder is not able to 
submit the certificate from practicing Chartered Accountant certifying its financial 
parameters, the audited results for the three (03) consecutive financial years preceding 
the last financial year shall be considered for evaluating the financial parameters. 
Further, a certificate would be required from CEO/CFO as per the format enclosed in the 
bidding document stating that the financial results of the company are under audit as on 
the date of Techno -commercial bid opening and the certificate from the practicing 
Chartered Accountant certifying financial parameters is not available.  
 
Notes for Clause 6.0 above: 
 
i. Net worth means the sum total of the paid -up share capital and free reserves. Free 
reserve means all reserves credited out of the profits and share premium account but 
does not include reserves credited out of the revaluation of the assets, write back of 
depreciation provision and amalgamation. Further any debit balance of Profit and Loss 
account and misce llaneous expenses to the extent not adjusted or written off, if any, 
shall be reduced from reserves and surplus.  
 
ii. Other income shall not be considered for arriving at annual turnover figures.  
 
iii. “Holding Company” and “Subsidiary Company” shall hav e the meaning ascribed to them 
as per Companies Act of India.
