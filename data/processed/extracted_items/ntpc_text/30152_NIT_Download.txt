2.0 BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK 
 
NTPC Ltd. intends to establish a Surface Coal Gasification (SCG) Project at Talaipalli, Raigarh, 
Chhattisgarh, for the production of 500 KTPA (Kilo Tonnes Per Annum) of Synthetic Natural 
Gas (SNG). The proposed plant will comprise the following technology islands: Coal 
Beneficiation, Coal Gasification, Syngas Conditioning & Cleaning, and Methanation. 
The overall project execution methodology shall be as follows: 
 Selection of the Technology Licensor through the technology evaluation process. 
 Preparation of the Basic Design Engineering Package (BDEP)  by the selected licensor 
based on the finalized technology. 
 Finalization of plant integration, utilities, and overall interface requirements using the 
BDEP as the basis. 
 Floating of a single EPC tender incorporating the BDEP and finalized integration and utility 
requirements, followed by selection of the EPC contractor for detailed engineering, 
procurement, construction, commissioning, and performance guarantee of the plant. 
 
The Methanation Unit is the first of the identified technology islands to be taken up for technology 
licensor selection, thereby initiating the preparation of the Basic Design Engineering Package (BDEP).  
 
 
The Methanation Unit shall convert the clean syngas received from the Syngas Cleanup/Conditioning 
Unit into Synthetic Natural Gas (SNG) meeting the specified product quality requirements for injection 
into the GAIL pipeline network.  
The Licensor shall establish the process design basis, validate the battery limit feed conditions, and 
ensure that the proposed process configuration is compatible with the overall plant integration and 
performance objectives. 
The scope of the package shall include: (i) Grant of Technology License, (ii) Basic Engineering 
Design Package (BDEP) and Post-BDEP Engineering Support, (iii) Supply of first-fill catalyst, and 
(iv) Commissioning and Performance Test Support.
