The bidder who wishes to participate in the bidding shall meet the Qualifying Requirements 
stipulated here under: 
 
A The bidder in the preceding Seven (07) years prior to the date of techno -commercial bid 
opening should have executed “Civil Construction works’ with, 
 
i)  Single (01) order of executed value not less than ₹ 42 lakhs (Rupees Forty-Two 
Lakhs) 
OR 
ii) Two (02) orders, each of executed value not less than ₹ 26 Lakhs (Rupees 
Twenty Six Lakhs) 
OR 
iii) Three (03) orders, each of executed value not less than ₹ 21 lakh (Rupees 
Twenty-One Lakhs) 
 
Note: Values indicated above are exclusive of taxes. 
Note: Execution of earth work / road works in isolation will not be considered. 
B The average annual turnover (AATO) of the bidder in the preceding three (03) financial years 
as on the date of techno- commercial bid opening, shall not be less than ₹ 53 Lakhs (Rupees 
Fifty-Three Lakhs). 
 
Notes: 
 
i) Other income shall not be considered for arriving at annual turnover.

commercial bid opening is not available, the financial results certified by a practicing
