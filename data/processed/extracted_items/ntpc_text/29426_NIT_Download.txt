4. BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK 
 
NTPC Limited plans to expand the capacity of: 
 
 Talcher Super Thermal Power Station (Talcher STPP) by adding two additional 800 MW units as 
part of Talcher STPP Stage-III.  
 
The proposed project is located near Kaniha Village of Angul district in Odisha. The nearest 
railhead is Talcher Railway Station which is at a distance of 23 km (aerial) from the power plant. 
NH-55 is passing nearby the plant connecting to Talcher. The nearest Airport is Bhubaneswar 
which is about 121 Km (aerial) from the power plant and the nearest seaport Paradip is 190 Km 
(aerial) from the power plant. 
 
The EIA study will be conducted through a NABET accredited consultant for ‘Thermal Power 
Sector’ as per MOEF&CC standard TOR & approved TOR covering all the disciplines of 
environment (Land Use, Water Use, Demography & Socio-economics, Geology, Soils, 
Sediments, Hydrology, Water Quality, Meteorology, Air Quality, Terrestrial Ecology, Aquatic 
Ecology and Noise) based on one season field monitoring as per EIA notification dated 
14.09.2006. Draft EIA Report will be prepared on the basis of one season (non-monsoon) 
baseline data and proposed project approved Term of References (TOR) for conducting Public 
Hearing (PH). 
 
 
The consultant will enable in presenting the findings of the EIA report before the Environmental 
Public Hearing Committee, OSPCB and Expert Appraisal Committee (EAC) of the MoEF&CC, 
and submit all clarifications/replies/study findings/technical queries etc. for obtaining Consent to 
Establish (CTE) from the OSPCB and Environment Clearance (EC) from MoEF&CC. The EIA 
Report shall be framed in accordance with the latest EIA Notification of MoEF&CC dated 
14.09.2006 or later and its subsequent amendments as applicable. 
 
 
The detailed scope of work shall be as per scope defined in the Vol-II, Terms of Reference (TOR) 
of Bidding Document.
