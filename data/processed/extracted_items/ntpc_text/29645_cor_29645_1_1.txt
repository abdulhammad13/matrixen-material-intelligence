SECTION-II: INFORMATION TO APPLICANTS  
 
 
 
 
Introduction 
 
NTPC Kanti is a power generating station with a total installed capacity of 390 MW, engaged in 
the generation and sale of bulk power. As part of its operations, NTPC Kanti produces 
approximately 7 lakh metric tonnes (LMT) of ash annually. Aligned with NTPC’s commitment to 
sustainable business practices and its vision of contributing to India’s growth, the plant promotes 
the effective utilization of ash by manufacturing eco-friendly bricks. NTPC Kanti currently has 
500,000 in-house (FQA Lab) tested bricks available for sale in the open market. Each brick 
measures 230 mm × 110 mm × 75 mm and fly ash brick weighs approximately 3.2 kg. Samples 
of the fly ash bricks have been tested as per IS 3495, demonstrating an average compressive 
strength of 75 kg/cm² and water absorption of less than 20%. Detailed test reports are available 
with the Ash Management Department of NTPC Kanti and may be referred to upon request. The 
bricks are offered at a provisional price of ₹6.15 per unit (exclusive of GST). However, the 
indicated price is subject to revision at NTPC’s sole discretion at the time of tendering. In case of 
any queries regarding fly ash bricks, the Ash Management Department of NTPC Kanti may be 
contacted through mail id: hodaukanti@ntpc.co.in.
