QUALIFYING REQUIREMENT 
Name of Work: Resurfacing/Re-carpeting work of approach Roads to PP-III at NSPCL-Bhilai 
 
 
1.0 Technical Requirements: 
1.1 The bidder should have executed contract for similar work(s) in the preceding seven (07) years; 
reckoned as on the date of technical bid opening with executed contract value(s) as below: 
 
a) Single order with executed value not less than Rs 135.65 lakh (exclusive of taxes and duties) 
OR 
b) Two orders with executed value not less than Rs 84.78 lakh each (exclusive of taxes and duties) 
OR 
c) Three orders with executed value not less than Rs 67.82 lakh each (exclusive of taxes and duties) 
 
2.0 Financial Requirement: 
2.1 The average annual turnover of the Bidder in the preceding three (03) financial years, reckoned 
as on the date of technical bid opening shall not b e less than Rs 169.56 lakh (Indian Rupees One 
crore sixty-nine lakh fifty-six thousand only exclusive of taxes and duties). 
 
Notes: 
a. The word “Executed” means the bidder should have achieved the criterion specified in Qualifying 
Requirements, even if the total contract is not com pleted/closed. A work executed in the preceding 
seven (7) years reckoned as on the date of technica l bid opening, even if it has been started earlier,  
will also be considered for meeting the requirement s against Clause 1.1. above. Agency has to 
furnish proof of executed value of the work in the form of copies of RA Bills, Relevant PO copy and 
client’s completion certificate/RA bill/Final Devia tion Statement etc. in support of meeting 
Qualifying Requirement. 
 
b. The phrase Similar Work(s) means “Construction/R epair of road(s) consisting of Pre-mix 
bituminous carpet and/or WBM and/or WMM and/or Bituminous Road Pre-mix bituminous carpet 
and/or RCC roads and/or minor bridge and culvert at any industrial organization/utility” 
 
c. In case where audited results for the last finan cial year as on the date of Technical Bid opening 
are not available, the financial results certified by a practicing Chartered Accountant shall be 
considered acceptable. In case, Bidder is not able to submit the Certificate from a practicing 
Chartered Accountant certifying its financial param eters, the audited results of three consecutive 
financial years preceding the last financial year s hall be considered for evaluating the financial 
parameters. Further, a Certificate would be required from the CEO/CFO/Proprietor/Partner as per 
the format enclosed in the bidding documents statin g that the financial results of the Company are 
under audit as on the date of Technical bid opening  and the Certificate from the practicing 
Chartered Accountant certifying the financial parameters is not available. 
 
d. Other income shall not be considered for arriving at annual turnover.
