Bid/ RA Section Seller Query/ Representation in GeM Portal NTPC Reply
Request to amend QR: We request removal of the NABH requirement in the clause “Successful 
operation (≥6 months) in ≥5 hospitals each ≥50 beds & NABH certified.” NABH accreditation pertains 
to hospital quality processes and is not related to HMIS performance or ABDM compliance. Many 
large public hospitals operating HMIS are not NABH certified, which unnecessarily restricts fair 
competition and does not reflect the true capability of the software. Request to allow ≥5 hospitals of 
≥50 beds irrespective of NABH status.
Regarding the clause “Implementation in ≥100-bed NABH hospital, including 1 year 
AMC/subscription” — NABH certification is for hospitals, not software. The requirement should be 
that the HMIS is NABH compliant, and not that the hospitals themselves are NABH certified.
