4 Brief scope of work 
1. Maintenance/ installation / Replacement / modification of signalling 
equipment such as track circuit, signals, Battery Bank, Point Machine, 
Universal Axle counters and associated equipments in Merry Go Round 
(MGR) System of NTPC Plant. 
2. The Maintenance work of signaling system would be done regularly as 
per Indian Railway Signal engineering manual unless otherwise specified 
in the technical specification of the tender document (RFQ). 
NB: Detailed Work Scope will be as per Subsequent Tenders only.  
5 Technical  Qualifying 
requirements

& Commissioning of Signaling & Telecommunication (S&T) system, in 
Indian Railways/PSU/Govt. Dept. /any other company having captive 
railway system, during last five (5) years from the date of application for 
enlistment.
