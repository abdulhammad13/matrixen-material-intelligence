36 Payment Terms — Warranty Retention (Clause 19.3, 
Page 11 of SOW)
As per Clause 19.3 of the SOW, a payment retention of 
5% is stipulated against warranty obligations. While we 
fully appreciate NTPC’s intent to safeguard project 
deliverables and performance, such a retention 
amount in a large-volume tender may have a significant 
impact on bidders’ working capital and overall project 
costing.
In this regard, we respectfully request NTPC to kindly 
consider allowing submission of a separate Bank Guarantee 
equivalent to the retention amount, in lieu of actual cash 
retention, over and above the Performance Security.
Alternatively, we request NTPC to consider releasing the 
retained amount on a yearly basis, as may be deemed 
appropriate by NTPC, so as to reduce the financial burden 
on bidders. Otherwise, the cost implication of retention is 
likely to be factored into the bid pricing.
Either of the above options would help bidders maintain 
healthier cash flows and reduce financing costs, while 
continuing to adequately address NTPC’s risk and warranty 
requirements.
No change. Tender terms  shall prevail
