10 Corrigendum 3, S. No 29/68 Fully Managed Data Warehouse 4 vCPU, 32 GB RAM for running 500 queries 
per month with each query scanning 1TB of data OR Serverless data 
warehouse for running 500 queries per month with each query
scanning 1TB of data
Technical Observation: The current specification of "4 vCPU, 32 GB RAM" 
describes a single-node instance that is technically incapable of functioning as 
an Enterprise Data Warehouse (EDW) for complex queries across large 
datasets. This configuration creates a biased bidding environment and poses a 
high risk of system failure under production loads. The current clause acts as a 
barrier for equal participation, limiting the pool of our qualified bidders.
Reason : To ensure the solution supports enterprise-grade analytics, the 
specifications should mandate a distributed architecture. We recommend clearly 
defining the 'Managed Data Warehouse' architecture within the RFP to prevent 
bidders from proposing a single VM configuration (e.g., 4 vCPU and 32 GB 
RAM). As industry-standard Data Warehouse engines require distributed 
resources for high-volume processing, a single-node specification would be 
technically inadequate and may inadvertently bias the procurement toward 
specific CSP services that do not meet NTPC’s long-term operational need
Propose Specs:
"a. Cloud-based Enterprise Data Warehouse: A distributed architecture 
consisting of at least 4 dedicated nodes (1 Leader Node and 3 Worker Nodes), 
with each node having a minimum of 4 vCPU and 32 GB RAM, capable of 
executing ~500 complex queries/month scanning at least 100GB per query at 
100% utilization.OR
b. Fully Managed Serverless Data Warehouse: A serverless model capable of 
executing complex queries (approx. 500 queries/month scanning minimum 
100GB per query) with automatic resource scaling to meet EDW performance 
requirements."
