DETAILED NOTICE INVITING EXPRESSION OF INTEREST (EOI) 
 
EOI No.: NUGEL/EOI-01/2026-27 Date: Mar 23, 2026 
 
NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) on behalf of  NTPC UP Green Energy Limited 
(NUGEL), is inviting expression of interest from industries, commercial establishments, 
DISCOMs, institutional buyers, and power aggregators in Uttar Pradesh for procurement of 
renewable energy power from its upcoming 100 MW Solar PV Project with 50 MW/ 200 MWh 
Battery Energy Storage System (BESS). 
 
Objective 
 
• To seek interest from prospective buyers with requirement of 5 MW and above. 
• To allow power aggregators to participate by clubbing smaller industries/establishments to 
achieve an aggregated demand of 5 MW or more. 
• To explore long-term power tie-up arrangements. 
• To offer power during solar generation hours as well as peak power supply backed by BESS 
during non-solar hours 
 
1. Eligibility Criteria 
The following entities shall be eligible to participate in this EoI: 
a) Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Consumers: Industries, commercial establishments, and 
institutional consumers having a power requirement of 5 MW or above 
b) Distribution Companies (DISCOMs): DISCOMs operating within the State of Uttar Pradesh 
c) Power Aggregators/Traders: Entities authorized to undertake power trading/aggregation, who 
can aggregate demand from multiple consumers to meet a minimum requirement of 5 MW. 
d) Legal Status: The applicant should be a legally registered entity under applicable laws and 
should be competent to enter into Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)/Power Sale Agreement. 
e) Power Procurement Readiness: The applicant should have, or be willing to obtain, necessary 
regulatory approvals and arrangements for power drawl under applicable regulations (including 
open access, where applicable). 
f) Compliance Declaration: The applicant should not be under any disqualification or 
blacklisting by any Central/State Government entity or regulatory authority.

Section - II 
Introduction and Background 
1. Introduction 
 
NUGEL is a joint venture company of NGEL (subsidiary of NTPC) & UPRVUNL (State owned 
GENCO of Uttar Pradesh). The company was incorporated on 01.01.2025 with the 
objective of establishing Solar projects up to 2 GW in the state of UP . NUGEL shall 
spearhead the green business initiatives of NTPC in the state of Uttar Pradesh and undertake 
projects that complement NTPC’s broader green energy journey towards achieving the 
ambitious target of 60 GW renewable capacity by FY 2032. 
 
The first project shall be 100 MW Solar PV Project integrated with 50 MW/200 MWh BESS 
located in the state of UP, ensuring reliable supply and ensured peak power availability. 
The project will be STU -connected to the nearest UPPTCL  substation, enabling robust grid 
integration and reliable evacuation of power. 
This initiative supports: 
• Uttar Pradesh’s renewable energy roadmap. 
• Industrial and commercial consumers seeking sustainable power solutions. 
• Participation of power aggregators to broaden access for smaller industries. 
• National objectives of energy transition and storage-backed renewables. 
 
The proposed 100 MW Solar project of NUGEL with 50% BESS / 200 M Wh is intended to 
supply power during both solar hours and non-solar hours with  firm, dispatchable power 
supply during non -solar hours, allowing industries and commercial establishments to 
reliably meet their peak demand requirements . By storing surplus solar energy generated 
during the day and releasing it during evening or high -demand periods, the project ensures 
assured power availability at affordable tariffs, reducing dependence on costly  grid power or 
backup diesel generation. This hybrid solar -plus-storage configuration not only enhances grid 
stability but also offers buyers the flexibility of renewable energy support, making it an 
attractive and future-ready solution for sustainable industrial growth in Uttar Pradesh. 
 
2. Purpose of the Expression of Interest (EOI) 
 
The primary objectives of this EOI are to: 
 
• Support NUGEL's vision to develop solar+ BESS projects in the state of UP. 
• To enter into long term power sale agreements assuring offtake of solar power by the 
upcoming solar project in UP

1. Responsiveness Check 
All submitted responses will be reviewed to confirm the applicant’s intent and eligibility for 
Power tie up in the state of UP.  Each response must comply with the specified submission 
requirements to be considered responsive. 
 
A response shall be considered non-responsive under the following conditions: 
 
a. Submission is incomplete, lacking any of the required formats such as the covering 
letter, Power of Attorney/Letter of Authorization, or relevant undertakings as outlined 
in Annexures of Section V. 
b. Response is not duly signed and stamped by the authorized signatory as required in this 
EOI. 
c. Presence of material inconsistencies or discrepancies in the submitted 
information/documents. 
d. An applicant submits multiple responses either directly or through an affiliate or 
subsidiary entity. 
e. The validity period of the response is less than that specified in Clause 4 of Section I 
of this EOI. 
 
f. The response is conditional in nature, containing deviations or qualifications. 
 
g. Submission is received after the deadline specified for EOI responses. 
 
h. Failure or delay in submitting additional information or clarifications requested by 
NGEL/NUGEL, where applicable. 
Only applications that satisfy all of the above criteria will be considered responsive and 
taken forward for further evaluation. NGEL/NUGEL reserves the sole discretion to 
deem a response non-responsive in case of any non-compliance, and such responses will 
be excluded from further consideration.
