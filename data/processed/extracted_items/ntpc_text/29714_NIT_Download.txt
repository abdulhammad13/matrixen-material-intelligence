2.0 BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The Bidder shall execute the project on a Lump Sum  basis, covering Licensing, Design, 
Engineering, Fabrication, Packing, Forwarding, Supply, Transportation and handling, Custom 
duty (and any other duties), Freight, Insurance, Loading & Unloading, Storage, Construction, 
Erection, Commissioning, Testing, PG Test, Training - in line with the specifications for the 
complete 150 TPD CO2 to Green Urea (CTGU) plant. 
 
The project comprises five (05) blocks of nominal capacity at two locations approx. 25km apart: 
325 TPD CO₂ Capture (at Simhadri), 15.3 TPD Hydrogen, ~70 TPD Nitrogen, 85 TPD Ammonia, 
and 150 TPD Urea Plant (at Pudimadaka).Scope includes all process units, utilities, offsites, 
interconnections, and balance of plant systems required for safe and reliable operation, including 
integration with NTPC facilities at specified terminal points. 
 
The Bidder shall design and provide all interfacing systems for tapping utilities from designated 
points, including all required materials, equipment, and services. Performance Guarantee (PG) 
tests shall be conducted to demonstrate guaranteed capacities and product quality. The Bidder 
shall obtain all statutory approvals and ensure compliance with applicable regulations (including 
Consent to Establish, Consent to Operate, Forest Clearance, EIA, QRA, etc.), and shall be 
responsible for safety management including deployment of safety personnel, provision of PPE, 
insurance, quality assurance, site execution, training, documentation, commissioning support, 
and supply of spares and consumables.
