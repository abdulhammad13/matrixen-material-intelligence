TECHNICAL CRITERIA OF QR: 
The applicant should have executed “Mechanical Erection/Overhauling/Mechanical 
Maintenance” Work of “Fans (ID/ FD/ PA) & Rotary Air -Preheater” in a coal based 
Thermal Power Station of India with Unit capacity of 200 MW or more during the 
preceding Five (5) years reckoned as on the date of submission of application.  
C Documents to be submitted by Applicant in support of meeting QR 
Relevant and legible PO copies with detailed scope of work, terms and conditions, BOQ 
POs (issued within last Five (5) years reckoned as on date of submission of application) 
in support of award and copy of Client’s Completion certificate/RA Bill/ final deviation 
statement from the concerned client /copies of invoice to establish successful execution as 
per QR.

G BRIEF 
SCOPE OF 
WORK 
 
A. Overhauling / Maintenance of Rotary Air-Preheaters: 
1. Internal washing of Air -Preheater and opening of all manholes and 
doors. 
2. Main Drive Gear Box servicing. 
3. Making Scaffolding at C.E. for Basket Removal. 
4. Fixing of hoist beam at H.E. Gas side for basket removal. 
5. Removal of baskets H.E., HIE & C.E. for high pressure jet cleaning. 
6. C.E. grating, Intermediate grati ng, rotor diaphragm, stay plate 
repairing/replacement. 
7. After washing and draining of water Baskets H.E., H.I.E. & C.E. 
placement  OR Replacement with new baskets. 
8. Repairing / Replacement of inboard & outboard static seal, Axial 
Seal Plate Assembly & filling of Kao wool. 
9. Dismantling / Repairing / Replacement of Air Seal Housing & filling 
of Kao wool to avoid flue gas leakage during running. 
10. Inspection of Guide Bearing, Support bearing & cleaning, box up & 
filling new oil. Replacement as per requirement. 
11. Repairing / Replacement of Rotor Angle &T-Bar. 
12. Sector Plate repairing/replacement/leveling H.E. & C.E. 
13. Fixing / setting of all seals. 
14. Repairing of inside Casing & Support. 
15. Checking / repairing/replacement of APH I/L & O/L Expansion 
joints. Fixing the Cover Plate on Expansion Joints. 
16. Checking the Soot Blower Lance Tube Assy. Movement etc. 
17. Checking & servicing of fire detection systems. 
18. Servicing of LOP’s of Support & Guide Bearings. 
19. Checking the Cooling Water Lines Valves. 
20. Shifting of material, erection of appro ach platform(s), removal of 
insulation & other related jobs as per site requirement. 
21. Other details shall be given as per site specific requirement in 
tender Documents. 
 
B. Overhauling / Maintenance of Fans: 
1. Cleaning of Fan Internals. 
2. Lube Oil system servicing. 
3. Servicing / Inspection / replacement of bearings (Sleeve / 
Antifriction Bearings as applicable) 
4. Servicing / inspection of inlet/outlet vanes/ blades/ Bearing 
assembly/ Blade bearing assembly/ Servomotor assembly/ all 
linkage and controls. 
5. Servicing / inspection/ rectification of Impeller /casing/ liners/ 
shafts/ couplings etc. and replacement as required. 
6. Alignment of Fans with drives (complete system) 
7. Assisting in Fans re-commissioning activities and attending any 
defects. 
8. Shifting of material, erection of approach platform(s), removal of 
insulation & other related jobs as per site requirement 
9.  Preventive maintenance / break down maintenance of Fans and its 
associated auxiliaries. 
10. Servicing of inlet/outlet connecting ducts up-to suction and 
discharge damper/gate. 
11. Assistance in balancing of fan, if required. 
12. Other details shall be given as per site specific requirement in 
tender Documents. 
 
C. Overhauling / Maintenance of Ducts  
1. Overhauling includes repair/ replacement of Ducts, Dampers, 
Gates, Insulation Removal, Expansion Joints, Diverter, Guide 
vanes, supports bracing etc. in Boiler area.  
2. Cleaning of ash from ducts. 
3. It includes activities like shifting of material, erection of platform, 
removal of insulation & other related jobs as per site requirement. 
4. Other details shall be given as per site specific requirement in 
tender Documents. 
 
 
D. HP Jet Cleaning of APH Baskets: 
The scope of work includes to and fro transportation of sufficient 
numbers of H.P. jet pumps with accessories from vendor’s site to 
respective locations. No separate payment will be done on this account. 
Party shall have to mobilize the pumps well in advance from the 
commencement of work (as intimated by NTPC). Cleaning of Air Pre-
heater basket shall cover: 
1. Transportation of pump and accessories at air heater floor or area 
where baskets are to be cleaned. 
2. Cleaning of all the baskets.  
3.  For above contractor shall deploy sufficient parallel gangs to carry 
out the cleaning work. Cleaning shall be certified by area engineer. 
Failure of proper cleaning work within stipulated time frame shall 
attract penalty as mentioned in tender enquiry. 
4. Other details shall be given as per site specific requirement in 
tender Documents. 
 
Note- 
1. The vendor must have at least Two nos. of Trolley mounted and 04 
nos. of portable HP jet cleaning machines. 
 
2. Execution of the work of "HP Jet cleaning of APH Baskets for 
Boiler" may be done by the Agency itself, only if it has the past 
experience of executing such works in a Boiler of minimum 200 MW 
capacity or these works may be outsourced to only those sub-vendors 
who have the past experience of executing such works in a Boiler of 
minimum 200 MW capacity." 
 
3. The approval for execution of Work by the Contractor himself or by 
his Subcontractor will be subjected to approval of EIC based on 
submission of experience details by the appointed Contractor for a 
particular package.
