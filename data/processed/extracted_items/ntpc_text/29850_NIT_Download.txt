2.0  BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK 
 The scope of work under this contract shall cover the following items of work. 
1. Grading and site levelling: The land within the project boundary has variable natural
ground levels. To make is workable, grading and levelling is required. The work involves
cutting the extra soil/ rock, filling the suitable soil at required area.
2. Site preparation and jungle clearance to make the land suitable for filling.
3. Road work from Main entrance to different locations are marked in the layout plan.
4. Construction of temporary drains to prevent water logging.
5. Streetlight for the road.
6. Laying of Hume pipe.
