2.0 Brief Scope of Work 
 
Design and detailed engineering, Procurement, Fabrication, Supply, Packing, Loading, Forwarding, 
Transportation, Unloading, Storage, Preservation, Freight, Insurance, Clearances, Custom or any other duty, 
Construction, Erection, Commissioning, Shop, Field Quality Tests and PG Test of 1 TPD Green Hydrogen 
plant including Mandatory spares supply of Feed Stock (MSW-RDF / Agri Waste) and 2 years O&M of the 
plant at NETRA, NTPC Greater Noida. 
 
3.0 NTPC intends to finance the aforesaid Package through a mix of debt and equity.
