Salient technical features: - 
Construction of pre -cast Boundary wall with the total height of minimum 2850mm above 
formation level Finished ground level in case formation level is less than natural ground level). 
The pre-stressed precast reinforced cement concrete columns shall be provided at spacing 
not more than 2200mm c/c. 
The grade of concrete for all pre-stressed precast panels and column, etc shall be of minimum 
M30 grade and Grade of foundation block shall be min M25 grade conforming to IS 456. 
The minimum diameter of the foundation shall not be less than 350mm and depth less 
than1000mm.
