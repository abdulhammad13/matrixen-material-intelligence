1.0 NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL) invites online bids from eligible bidders on Single Stage 
Two Envelope (i.e. Envelope -I: Techno -Commercial Bid and Envelope -II: Price Bid) basis for 
Development of 132 kV GIS along with 80 MVA, 132/33 kV Power Transformer, 33 kV HT and 
415 V LT Switchgears for NGEL Green Hydrogen Hub at Pudimadaka, Andhra Pradesh. 
 
2.0 BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The scope includes the following: 
 
 1.1 The bidder shall be responsible for the design, engineering, supply, construction, erection,   
testing, commissioning of the 132/33 kV, 2 x 80 MVA sub-station comprising of 132 kV GIS, Power 
transformers, 33 kV HT switchgear and 415 V LT switchgear, automatic PF correction devices 
including civil and architectural works with 5 years of operation and maintenance (O&M) works 
of all systems on turnkey basis.  
 1.2 Completion of all facilities including commissioning of the 132 kV GIS, Power Transformer, 
Switchgears and its associated equipment’s should be completed within one year1 from the date 
of placement of award. 
 1.3 The equipment, system, documentation as below, are in the scope of bidder:  
a. 132 kV GIS of 5 bays along with metering, protection, SAS and communication system 
housed in the prefabricated container.  
b. Power Transformer of 2 x 80 MVA , 132/33 kV along with interconnecting gas insulated 
duct and terminal.  
c. 33 kV HT switchgear of 14 feeders and 415 V LT switchgears along with ACDB and MLDB 
housed in the prefabricated container.  
d. Auto PF correction of min. 2 x ±15 MVAr (Static VAR Gen.) device at 33 kV level.  
e. Firefighting facilities and Fire suppression system in GIS, power transformers, 
switchgears and control room including fire, gas detection and ventilation systems.

2.0 Financial criteria: 
 
2.1. The average annual turnover of the bidder should not be less than Rs. 49 Crore (INR Forty-
Nine Crore only) or in equivalent foreign currency during the preceding three financial years 
as on the date of techno-commercial bid opening. 
In case the Bidder does not satisfy the average annual turnover criteria, stipulated at Clause 
2.1 on its own, its Holding Company would be required to meet the stipulated turnover 
requirements as above, provided that the Net Worth of such Holding Company as on the last 
day of the preceding financial year is at least equal to or more than the paid-up share capital 
of the Holding Company. In such an event, the bidder would be required to furnish along with 
the technocommercial bid, a Letter of Undertaking from the Holding Company, supported by 
the Holding Company’s Board Resolution, as per the format enclosed in the bid documents, 
pledging unconditional and irrevocable financial support for the execution of the Contract by 
the Bidder in case of an award. 
2.2. Net Worth of the Bidder as on the last day of the preceding financial year should not be less 
than 100% (hundred percent) of the bidder’s paid -up share capital. In case the Bidder does 
not satisfy the Net Worth criteria on its own, it can meet the requirement of Net worth based 
on the strength of its Subsidiary (ies) and/ or Holding Com pany and/or Subsidiaries of its 
Holding companies wherever applicable, the Net worth of the Bidder and its Subsidiary (ies) 
and/ or Holding Company and/ or Subsidiary (i es) of the Holding Company, in a combined 
manner should not be less than 100% (one hundred percent) of their total paid -up share 
capital. However individually, their Net worth should not be less than 75% (seventy -five 
percent) of their respective paid-up share capitals. Net worth in a combined manner shall be 
calculated as follows:
