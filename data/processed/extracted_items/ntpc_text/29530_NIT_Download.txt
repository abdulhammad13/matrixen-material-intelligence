Scope of Work:  
1. The scope of work includes cutting of grass, jungle, elephant grass, and bushes, along with 
disposal at locations specified by the EIC, as per the BOQ. The work may also be executed 
in areas outside the  plant premises, including near gate complex or any other location as 
directed by the EIC. 
2. The contractor shall arrange all required manpower, tools, tackles,  and equipment at their 
own cost. The contractor shall mobilize adequate resources to ensure completion of the work 
within the stipulated time schedule to the satisfaction of the EIC. 
3. Safety shall be of paramount importance, and the agency shall ensure pro vision and use of 
all necessary PPEs (safety shoes, helmets, gum boots, gloves, etc.) by the workforce. 
4. All arrangements for dwelling facilities to be provided by the Agency. 
5. Adequate arrangements shall be made by the contractor to facilitate inspection and checking 
of the work by the Engineer-in-Charge. 
6. Disposal of cut grass shall be carried out at low-lying areas identified by the EIC. 
7. Loading, unloading including disposal of grass with tractor trolley is included in the rate of 
item of grass/jungle cutting itself. 
8. The contractor shall deploy sufficient manpower to ensure that the areas assigned by the EIC 
are cleared within the contract period, including disposal. 
 
 
Special Terms and conditions:  
1. The contract period shall be 04 months, and the work shall be completed within the stipulated 
time, keeping in view safety and aesthetic requirements. However, period may be further 
extended for execution of balance PO quantity if required, on the recommendation of EIC. 
2. GST shall be payable extra, as applicable, for GST-registered vendors. 
3. Time shall be the essence of the contract. The agency shall mobilize within 15 days from the 
date of issue of LOI, or as directed by the EIC. 
4. Payments shall be made through monthly RA bills based on joint measurement records. 
5. The contractor shall strictly adhere to all applicable safety norms during execution of the 
work. 
6. The contractor shall obtain an appropriate labour insurance policy covering risks involved 
in grass cutting prior to commencement of work. 
7. The contractor shall deploy an experienced supervisor to monitor the work and participate 
in joint measurements. 
8. Insurance coverage for all labour engaged shall be ensured before commencement of work, 
and copies of the policies shall be submitted to the Engineer-in-Charge. 
9. The agency shall ensure timely payment of wages and statutory benefits to all manpower 
deployed. 
10. Wages shall be paid at not less than the minimum wages as notified by BRBCL from time 
to time.
