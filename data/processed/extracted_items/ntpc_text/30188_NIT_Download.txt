General Details about NTPC Nabinagar:   
NTPC Nabinagar, located at Auranagabad Distt Bihar, having a total installed capacity of 1980 
MW, is engaged in the business of generation and sale of bulk power.   
To utilize the fly ash in gainful and sustainable manner as per the provisions of extant MOEF&CC 
Gazette Notification and promote utilization of fly ash as resource material, NTPC Nabinagar 
intends to issue some of its Pond Ash on free of cost to Road Projects / Flyover embankments / 
Dams / Mines / Shoreline Protection structures in coastal Districts / Low Lying (as per extant 
MoEF&CC Notification and policy). Transportation cost (to the extent requested by the interested 
Ash User – with the pr ovision that agency requesting the least support shall be given higher 
preference) shall be borne by NTPC Nabinagar within limits and as per criteria set by extant 
Government Notification, Advisory and Policy. Preference shall be given to requests having 
minimum cost implication to NTPC for ash transportation.

Gazette Notification dated 31.12.2021 on Fly Ash Utilization to the agencies engaged in 
construction activities such as road laying, road and flyover embankments, shoreline protection 
structures in coastal districts and dams, Low Lying, Mine Owners for Mine backfilling and OB 
Mixing, within 300 kms radius, for a period of 01 (One) year, from its 3 X 660 MW Stage-1 units

Part 4: Terms and Conditions and General Guidelines:  
1. Pond Ash shall be issued from the designat ed area of Ash 
Dyke(s) / Pond(s) or other location as decided by EIC to the Ash Users 
(or their authorized representatives as may be agreed to in writing by 
the EIC), considering Operational and Safety issues of Ash dyke.  
2. Statutory / regulatory compliances shall be binding upon all the 
Ash Users and verifiable by TPP.   
3. Interested Ash User shall make themselves aware of the various 
extant Rules, Procedures, Safety norms, Environmental and 
Sustainability Guidelines etc and carry out their activities in full 
compliance with the same.  
4. No person / agency shall be allowed to lift ash without due 
permission from TPP.  
5. Depending upon operational convenience, safety, etc. EIC can 
schedule the lifting of Ash from Designated storage area by different 
agencies, which shall be binding upon the agencies. The schedules 
and deployment m ay be worked out / altered based on periodic 
meetings between concerned stakeholders.  
6. On allocation of Pond Ash against this Notice, Successful Ash 
Users shall submit affidavit on Non – judicial stamp paper of requisite 
value, certifying the complete Util ization of issued Pond Ash, in 
adherence with the provisions of the extant Notification and other 
applicable statutory regulations. This affidavit, in prescribed format, 
shall be submitted on Half Yearly basis to the EIC.   
7. In case of any liability on TPP, in any manner that arises due to 
non-compliance of any Environmental and Transportation Norms, 
Rules, Guidelines and Statutory provisions by the Ash User or its 
representative / agent, during the course of execution and  for future 
obligations (if any) by  way of omission or commission, the Ash User 
shall keep TPP effectively indemnified of any such liability. An 
indemnity bond shall be submitted before start of lifting of Pond Ash.  
8. Interested and eligible Ash Users executing Project shall have 
to submit tentative time schedule of the project along with Request.  
9. Interested and eligible Ash User shall have to submit End User 
Certificate for utilization of supplied Pond Ash.  
10. All Eligible Projects shall source Pond Ash from Thermal Power 
Plants (TPPs) closes t to their Project. Allocation of Pond Ash by TPP 
shall be in compliance with the same.  
11. It shall also be the responsibility of the Ash User(s) to ensure 
that the Ash sent from the TPP is facilitated for quick unloading and 
release of vehicle.
