SCOPE OF WORK 
NTPC invites applications from eligible vendors for enlistment 
or updating of credentials of already enlisted vendors  to be 
used for the future tenders of Coal Transportation for NTPC 
Coal Mines as per the requirement of specific tenders having 
scope of work as brought out below. The enlisted eligible 
vendors shall be considered for the various tenders as per their 
execution capability defined under enlistment document 
hereafter.  
 
The scope of work of NTPC tenders will generally include 
Transportation of Coal from Source Location (such as Mines, 
Stock Yard or from any other source) to Destination (Railway 
Siding, Projects, stock Yard or any other destination) as 
informed by EIC including work of Loading of Coal into tippers 
by mechanical means, Transporting of Coal,  stacking and 
arrangement of the siding , putting tarpaulin over loaded 
wagon/trucks, Rake indenting, Railway siding yard management 
, Liaison with Railway, Rake loading  & unloading (if required) by 
pay loader of minimum specified capacity, high -capacity dozer 
and other HEMM equipment including ensuring quantity of coal 
at Source and destination etc. as per the requirement of 
specific tenders and complying to statutory norms and 
government rules. Suitable arrangements have to be made by 
the Agency for transportation of Coal in an environmentally 
friendly manner. Agency must ensure that no spillage of coal 
takes place during transportation. 
 
Note: The detail scope of work & other Terms & conditions 
would be specified separately in the Tender against the specific 
requirements.

7.) Criteria for Enlistment and 
Participation in a Tender  
 
(7.1 Execution capability 
based on  technical 
criteria 
 
and 
 
7.2 Execution capability 
based upon  financial 
requirement) 
The enlisted applicants can participate in a particular tender 
either individually (i.e. Route -1) or by forming a consortium 
(i.e. Route-2) with other enlisted applicants (maximum three 
entities are allowed in a consortium).  
 
The eligibility for participation in a particular tender shall be 
as per their Execution Capability based on Technical Criteria 
and their average annual turnover of the preceding  3 years 
arrived based on data submitted at the time of enlistment. 
 
Definitions of terms for eligibility criteria for a particular 
tender: 
 
CET: Cost Estimate of Tender 
CP: Completion period in months 
EC: Execution Capability based on Technical Criteria (as per 
enlistment)          
AATO: Average Annual Turn Over of preceding 3 years as per 
enlistment. 
 
EC and AATO shall be arrived as under: 
 
I. In case the enlisted applicant participates in a 
tender individually (Route-1) 
 
EC =   2 x Cumulative Value of three highest value 
work orders executed in any year (consecutive 365 
days) during last 7 (seven) years reckoned from the 
last date of submission of application. 
 
Illustration(s): 
1) If cumulative value of work executed by 
applicant from maximum three Purchase 
Order/Work Order/Agreement in any year 
(consecutive 365 days) is Rs. 2 Crore, then, its 
execution capability (EC) will be considered as 
2x2=Rs. 4 Crore. 
 
2) If cumulative value of work executed by 
applicant from maximum three Purchase 
Order/Work Order/Agreement in any year 
(consecutive 365 days) is Rs. 20 Crore, then, its 
execution capability (EC) will be considered as 
2x20=Rs. 40 Crore. 
 
AATO = The average annual turnover of the 
applicant, in the preceding three (3) financial years 
as on the last date of submission of application 
(Applicants are required to meet minimum net -
worth criteria without which their application shall 
not be considered for enlistment) 
 
 
 
Minimum Net Worth Criteria for Route-1:  
 
Net worth of the Applicant as on the last day of the preceding 
financial year (as on the last day of submission of application) 
should not be less than 100% of the Applicant’s paid up share 
capital.  
 
In case the Applicant does not satisfy the Net worth criteria on 
its own, it can meet the requirement of Net worth based on the 
strength of its Subsidiary(ies) and/or Holding Company and/or 
Subsidiary(ies) of its Holding Companies wherever 
applicable. The net worth of the Applicant and its 
Subsidiary(ies) and/or Holding Company and/or 
Subsidiary(ies) of the Holding Company, in combined manner 
should not be less than 100% of their total paid -up share 
capital. However individually, their Net worth should not be 
less than 75% of their respective paid-up share capitals. 
 
 
Net worth in combined manner shall be calculated as follows: 
 
Net worth (combined) = (X1+X2+X3) / (Y1+Y2+Y3) X 100% 
 
Where X1, X2, X3 are individual Net worth which should not 
be less than 75% of their respective paid -up share capitals and 
Y1, Y2, Y3 are individual paid up share capitals. 
 
II. In case the enlisted applicants form consortium 
among themselves (maximum three entities) for 
participation in a particular tender (Route-2) 
EC of the Route -2 bidder (i.e. Consortium) shall 
be the lowest of the values arrived as under: 
 
(a) EC of each Consortium Member =    
 
10 x Highest value of work executed in a 
single work order in any year (consecutive 
365 days) during last 7 (seven) years 
reckoned from the last date of submission of 
application. 
 
(b) EC of Consortium = 2 x Cumulative Value of 
three highest value work orders executed in 
any year (consecutive 365 days) during last 7 
(seven) years reckoned from the last date of 
submission of application 
 
 
 
 Illustration(s): 
  
I. If highest value of single work order executed in any year 
(consecutive 365 days) during last 7 (seven) years 
reckoned from the last date of submission of application by 
consortium member-1 is Rs 4 crore, consortium member -2 
is Rs 6 crore, consortium member -3 is Rs 8 crore then EC 
shall be arrived as under: 
 
EC of consortium member -1 is Rs 40 crore (i.e. 4 x 10), 
consortium member -2 is Rs 60 crore (i.e. 6 x 10), 
consortium member-3 is Rs 80 crore (i.e. 8 x 10), and EC 
of consortium is Rs 36 crore [i.e. 2 x (4+ 6 + 8)]. 
 
Based on the above, EC of Route -2 applicant shall be Rs 
36 crores (i.e. lowest EC value) 
 
II. If highest value of single work order executed in any year 
(consecutive 365 days) during last 7 (seven) years 
reckoned from the last date of submission of application by 
consortium member-1 is Rs 3 crore, consortium member -2 
is Rs 12 crore, consortium member -3 is Rs 25 crore then 
EC shall be arrived as under: 
 
EC of consortium member -1 is Rs 30 crore (i.e. 3 x 10), 
consortium member -2 is Rs 120 crore (i.e. 12 x 10), 
consortium member-3 is Rs 250 crore (i.e. 25 x 10), and 
EC of consortium is Rs 80 crore [i.e. 2 x (3+ 12 + 25)].  
 
Based on the above, EC of Route -2 applicant shall be Rs 
30 crores (i.e. lowest EC value) 
 
AATO = The average annual turnover of each member of 
the consortium, in the preceding three (3) financial years as 
on the last date of submission of application shall be added 
together to arrive at the average annual turnover of the 
consortium (each member of the consortium as well as 
consortium as a whole are required to meet minimum net -
worth criteria without which their application shall not be 
considered for enlistment) 
 
Minimum Net Worth criteria for Route -2: Net worth of 
all consortium/ JV members in combined manner should 
not be less than 100% of their paid up share capital 
however individually, their Net worth should not be less 
than 75% of their respective paid up share capitals. 
 
 
 
 Notes for Route-2 participation: 
i) The start date and end date of works claimed for 
qualifying experience may be of same or different periods 
(consecutive 365 days) for different consortium members. 
ii) All the Consortium members shall select one of the 
members as the “Leader”. 
iii) Each of the Consortium members will be required to 
furnish a legally enforceable Consortium Operating Agreement 
(COA) in a particular tender holding themselves jointly & 
severally responsible and liable to NTPC to perform all 
contractual obligations, valid for the entire period of contract, as 
per the format enclosed in the bidding documents. The number 
of executants of the COA should not exceed three. 
Based on the EC and AATO arrived above, bidder shall be 
eligible for participation in a particular tender provided they 
meet the following criteria: 
1) For the tenders having completion period ≤ 1 years 
AATO ≥ CET and EC ≥ CET 
2) For the tenders having completion period > 1 year but less 
than 2 years 
AATO ≥ CET X12/CP and EC ≥ CET 
3) For the tenders having completion period ≥ 2 years 
AATO ≥ CET X12/CP and EC ≥ CET x12/CP 
 
Note: No enlisted applicant is permitted to submit more than 
one bid in a particular tender.

2.0 INTRODUCTION 
 
NTPC Limited is the largest power generating company of India. It was incorporated  in 
the year 1975, with the objective of planning, promoting and organizing an integrated 
development of power stations in India. It has today emerged as an ‘Integrated Power 
Major’, with a significant presence in the entire value chain of power  generation 
business. 
 
NTPC is developing 7 coal blocks (including Banhardih coal block of PVUNL), for captive 
consumption of the coal. The total peak rated annual production capacity of these coal blocks is 
estimated to be 71 MMT to fuel approximately 15 GW power production of NTPC. 
 
NTPC plans for enlistment of eligible vendors meeting the specified eligibility criteria 
and willing to be associated with NTPC for  the future tenders of Coal Transportation for 
NTPC Coal Mines as per the requirement of specific tenders. 
 
The intent of this enlistment is to identify and enlist prospective vendors / contractors 
who meet Technical Qualifying Requirements and financial parameters as explained in 
Section-II QR & SOW enclosed herewith. This enlistment will be applicable for all NTPC 
Coal Mines. Vendor enlistment process shall be a continuous process with the option to 
add / delete/ modify the list of eligible vendors . L e f t  o u t  v endors, desirous to get 
their names enlisted/ updated in the approved list, can do so in the subsequent cycle  
only. 
 
 
3.0 SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The scope of work of NTPC tenders will generally include loading coal into Tippers by 
mechanical means & transportation from the Surface Coal Stock yard / Stacker Re -
claimer / any other stockpile of Coal Mining Projects as directed by EIC (Engineer in 
charge) up to the specified destination. Coal is also to be loaded into the railway wagons 
by pay loader of minimum specified capacity, high -capacity dozer and other HEMM 
equipment. The scope may also include maintenance of Railway Siding & Associated 
Miscellaneous Works as per the requirement of specific tenders and complying to 
statutory norms and government rules. 
 
Suitable arrangements must be made by the Agency for transportation of coal in an 
environmentally friendly manner. Agency must ensure that no spillage of coal takes 
place during transportation. 
NTPC LIMITED 
 
Quantity Measurement and Payment Criterion will be specified separately in the Tender 
against the specific requirements.
