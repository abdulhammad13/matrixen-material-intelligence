5.0 Technical Criteria 
 
5.1 The bidder should have executed “Building Interior Work(s) which includes 
works of interior acoustics” within preceding seven (07) years reckoned as on 
the date of Techno commercial bid opening meeting the following criteria: - 
 
i. Single order having executed value of above work(s) not less than Rs 98 
Lakhs. 
 
OR 
 
ii. Two orders having executed value of above work(s) not less than Rs 61 
Lakhs each. 
 
OR 
 
iii. Three o rders having executed value of above work(s) not less than Rs 49 
Lakhs each. 
 
Notes for Clause 5.1 above: 
 
I. For 5.1 above, one reference work shall mean the work executed at one premises/ 
campus through a single contract. 
 
II. For 5.1 above, HVAC System, fire A larm, fire Protection, fire -fighting System, 
Interior work, associated Electrical Works, Audio -Visual System, Furniture, 
Furnishing, Plumbing Work, Stage Lighting Work if forms part of the reference 
work, the same shall also be considered for arriving at the cost of the work. 
 
III. The word “Executed" mentioned above means that the bidder should have 
achieved the cr iteria specified above, even if the total contract is started earlier 
and/or is not completed/closed. 
 
IV. In case of orders under execution, the value o f work executed prior to the date of 
Techno-Commercial bid opening duly certified by bidder’s client shall be 
considered acceptable. 
 
V. Reference work executed by the bidder as a sub -contractor may also be 
considered provided the certificate issued by main c ontractor is duly certified by 
owner specifying the scope of work executed by the sub -contractor in support  of 
qualifying requirements. 
 
VI. For arriving at the executed value of work specified above, basic amount only shall 
be considered. In case contract is inclusive of taxes, bidder should provide the 
break – up of basic value and taxes.
