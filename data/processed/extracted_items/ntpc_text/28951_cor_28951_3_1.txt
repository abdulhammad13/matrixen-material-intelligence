However, industrial/corporate film is defined below: - 
 
Industrial/Corporate Film i s a form of audio-visual 
communication tool which use s facts/data related to the 
organization as available in the public domain and 
creatively presents them through the use of graphics, 
animation, voice over etc for internal/external stakeholders 
to drive positive narrative and generate goodwill for the 
company/organization. 
This can be long or short video format and may be simple 
documentary (with voice over) or may use storytelling with 
the help of artists. 
Corporate Films are primarily produced by in-house 
communication teams with the  help of external agencies 
and are meant for non-commerc ial advertisement of the 
organization/company/corporate. 
In the context of NTPC, we have been utilising Corporate 
Film for our branding/publicity activity wherein inputs 
regarding our core business along with other initiatives are 
comprehensively captured to amplify positive narrative 
around NTPC targeting the larger audience. 
In contrary, TVC is a short fil m capsule/video spot, which 
may be fictional or dramatized, to portray the brand in a 
positive light and have commercial implications as TVCs 
are basically commercial advertisements aired on TV 
networks.
