4. BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK
NTPC is proposing for expansion of Tanda Super Thermal  Power Project St age-II (2x660 MW)  
from capacity of 1320 MW to 2920 MW by adding two units of 800 MW as Stage-II (2x800 MW). 
Bahadurgarh village, Tanda Taluk i n the Ambedkar Nagar district of Utta r Pradesh. The site  is 
located at a n aerial dist ance of about 6 km from Tanda (East), approx. 20 kms from Akbarpur  
(South-East) and about 60 km from Ayodhya (Nort h-West). National Highway 28 passes in East  
direction at an approx. distance of 0.7 km from site. St ate Highway no. 30 A passes in South  
adjacent to the  site. The nearest airstrip exist s at Akbarpur an arial distance of 16 kms and  
nearest commercial airport- is located in Ayodhya at an a pprox. distance of 60 km from project 
site.The site has latitude and longitude of about 26 o 35’ 10’’ N to 82o 35’ 37’’ E.
The EIA study wi ll be conducted through a N ABET accredited consultant for ‘ Thermal Power  
Sector’ as per M oEF&CC standard TOR & approved TOR covering a ll the disciplines of  
environment (Land Use, Water Use, Demography & Socio-economics, Geology, Soils, Sediments, 
Hydrology, Water Quality,  Meteorology,  Air Quality, Terrestrial Ecology,  Aquatic Ecology and  
Noise) based on one  sea son(Non-monsoon) field monitoring as per EIA notification da ted 
14.09.2006 and subsequent amendments. Draft EIA Report will be prepared on the basis of one 
season (non-monsoon) baseline data and proposed project approved Term of References (TOR) 
for conducting Environmental Public Hearing (PH).  
 
The consultant will enable in presenting the findings of the EIA study report before the 
Environmental Public Hearing Committee, UPPCB and Expert Appraisal Committee (EAC) of the 
MoEF&CC, and submit all clarifications/replies/study findings/technical queries etc. for 
obtaining Consent to Establish (CTE) from the UPPCB and Environment Clearance (EC) from 
MoEF&CC. The EIA Report shall be framed in accordance with the latest EIA Notification of 
MoEF&CC dated 14.09.2006 and its subsequent amendments as applicable. 
 
The detailed scope of work shall be as per scope defined in Volume-II-Terms of Reference 
(TOR) of Bidding Document.
