Part 1:  General Information 
General Details about NTPC SIPAT:  
NTPC Sipat (2980 MW), a leading power generating unit of the largest power utility of India, 
NTPC Ltd, is located in the Bilaspur District of the state Chhattisgarh.  
NTPC Sipat is at a distance of approximately 25 KM from Bilaspur Railway station and is well 
connected by road. 
NTPC Sipat, generates approximately 55-60 Lakh MT of Ash annually as a byproduct of coal 
fired power generation plant. NTPC Sipat has total 9 Nos silos for dry fly ash. The silos are 
capable of loading dry fly ash in the conditioned (with desired moisture content) form. 
To summarize: 
02 silos of capacity 1850 MT each are located outside main plant area and are available 
for Road Transport only.  
03 silos of 750 MT each are located inside main plant area and are available for Road and 
as well as Rail transport (BOXN type wagons). 
04 silos of 1,500 MT each are located inside main plant area and are available for Road 
and as well as Rail transport (BOXN/BTAP type wagons). 
To utilize the Dry Fly Ash in gainful and sustainable manner as per the provisions of extant 
MOEF&CC Gazette Notification and promote utilization of fly ash as resource material, NTPC 
SIPAT intends to issue some of its Dry Fly Ash to interested agencies on free of cost and on first 
come first served basis, where transportation cost shall be borne by the bidder. 
For this purpose, NTPC Sipat invites Expressions of Interest from interested agencies, for issuing 
the ash from above mentioned silos for a period of 01 (One) year.

Allocation of Dry Fly Ash Quantity: 
Based on the time of receipt of responses to EOI, Dry Fly Ash shall be allocated to various bidders 
free of cost  and on first come first served basis, till the available offered quantity is exhausted .  
However, collecting ash from NTPC`s designated location and transportation thereafter along with 
other arrangements, shall be in the scope of the bidder only. 
In case the same Bidder gets some allocation against both the Auction Process and this EoI, then 
the Ash quantity allocated against this EOI shall be issued to that Bidder, only after the  Bidder 
completes the lifting /drawl of the full allocation against the Auction Process 
Daily allocation of Dry Fly Ash shall be done as given below:

adjusted SD amount will be refunded within 30 days of end of the contract period or of lifting of 
entire allocated quantity, whichever is earlier.

Part 4 (A): Terms and Conditions and General Guidelines: 
1. Dry fly ash shall be issued from the Ash Silo or other location as decided by EIC to 
the Successful Bidders (or their authorized representatives as may be agreed to in 
writing by the EIC), considering Dry Ash Evacuation System (DAES) Availability, 
Operational and Safety issues.  
2. Bidders shall make themselves aware of the various extant Rules, Procedures, 
Safety norms, Environmental and Sustainability Guidelines etc and carry out their 
activities in full compliance with the same.  
3. Transportation of Dry Fly Ash shall be done in completely covered trucks by 
200GSM Tarpaulin. Any spill of ash, ash mixed water from the transporting vehicles 
shall be cleaned off by the transporter at his own cost. 
4. No person / agency shall be allowed to lift ash without due and written permission 
from the EIC. Such cases shall be liable to prosecution as per legal proceedings 
5. Transporter’s vehicles/drivers shall follow the prescribed speed limit, routes and 
other SOPs provided by EIC for the transportation.  
6. Bidders eligible for collecting dry fly ash shall apply for permission for the 
bulkers/covered trucks that they propose to deploy and submit the same to EIC 
along with specified documents like registration, license, insurance etc., who shall 
arrange to ver ify the same and issue permission as per prevailing procedure 
adopted at the Station.  
7. Depending upon operational convenience, safety, etc. EIC can schedule or 
reschedule the lifting of ash from Ash Silos by different agencies, which shall be 
binding upon the Successful Bidders. Successful Bidders shall start collecting the 
Ash as per their respective allocation as per the schedule intimated by the EIC.  
8. Statutory / regulatory compliances shall be binding upon all the Bidders and 
verifiable by TPP.  
9. Non – compliance shall attract penal actions ranging from fine to termination of 
contract / agreement / issue of Dry Fly ash as decided by EIC  
10. Transportation of Ash shall be in the Scope of the Successful Bidder(s).  
11. Road Permits, Operator / Driver Licenses, Insurance etc. for the deployed Vehicles 
shall be submitted at the time of start of work (and also when new and / or additional 
vehicles are deployed) and thereafter as and when demanded by the EIC for 
verification. The vehicles shall be in fit condition for safe operation.  
12. Successful Bidders shall submit affidavit on Non – judicial stamp paper of requisite 
value, certifying the complete Utilization of issued Dry Fly ash, in adherence with 
the provisions of the extant Notification and other applicable statutory regulations. 
This affidavit, in prescribed format, shall be submitted on Half Yearly basis to the 
EIC.
