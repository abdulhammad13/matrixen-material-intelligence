4 BRIEF SCOPE OF WORK  The brief scope of work will generally include: - 
A) Unloading of Coal from the MGR/IR, MGR & IR BOBR/BOXN Rakes at 
unloading stations viz. track hoppers and wagon tipplers. Generally, BOBR type 
Wagons are unloaded at Track hoppers by bottom opening of doors, BOXN type 
wagons are placed at wagon tipplers for tippling. 
B) Further, manual unloading of these rakes needs to be carried out as per site 
requirement & facilities as and when required.  
C) Engaging assistance manpower (Non-Railway): - Diploma/HSK/SSK/USK 
staff in Control rooms and Silos, pointsman/shuntsman, Gatemen, Weigh bridge 
operators, Data entry operators, Patrolling staff etc. 
D) Staff for Housekeeping, Coal Cleaning, Tarpaulin removal from covered 
BOXN Wagons etc, 
E) Assistance in handling and placement of other IR Rakes viz. HFO/LDO Rakes, 
Ash Rakes. 
F) Deployment of vehicles with driver - Tractor with Trolley/JCB/Poclain/Jeep 
etc. In the case of NTPC owned vehicles, vehicle operators to be deployed for 
operating the same. 
G) T&P and consumables for MGR Operation.  
5 TECHNICAL Qualifying 
Requirement 
 
TECHNICAL CRITERIA: 
The applicant should have executed the works which must include ‘Loading of 
coal into/unloading of coal from’ any type of Bottom Opening Rapid discharge 
wagon (BOBR type)/ Bogie Open Wagon with Pneumatic Brakes (BOXN type) 
Rakes, within the preceding five (5) years from the last date of submission of 
application. 
Note for technical criteria: “Loading of coal into/unloading of coal from’ any type 
of ‘BOBR/BOXN rakes” means – “Manual/Mechanized Loading/unloading of 
BOBR/BOXN rakes”.
